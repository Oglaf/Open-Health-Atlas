Privacy

Sign-in is passkey (WebAuthn) only, and every account has its own separate data file. This panel cannot write a health file directly: every change (logging, notes, imports) goes through a validated health.py command over a local broker socket, never raw SQL. Your data is not sent off-device by this app. The connected sources above read in. If you connect an MCP client (see Desktop help & AI connection), that client and its model provider see whatever the client reads from your account.
