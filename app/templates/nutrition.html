How to set up targets

Check the engine reason above against these separate prerequisites. More than one may be missing:

  • Profile: height, sex and date of birth.
  • Weight: a body-weight record on or before today.
  • Configuration: micronutrient targets, phase offsets and protein targets.

Set your profile, diet phase, and daily kcal/protein overrides from Profile & targets. Record weight through the supported log body_metrics command or an importer.

Micronutrient targets, phase offsets, and protein-per-kg are also editable from Profile & targets for a family member account. The bootstrap owner account reads these three from its installation configuration instead (fork owner decision 2026-10-29, docs/PRODUCT_AUTHORITY.md) — set them with the OpenHealthAtlas command-line toolkit's HERMES_NUTRIENT_TARGETS_JSON/HERMES_PHASE_OFFSETS_JSON/HERMES_PROTEIN_PER_KG_JSON environment variables, or ask an admin to create a separate family member account.

After saving, run nutrition-targets (or just reload this page) to check the result.
